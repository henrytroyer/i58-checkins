Reflection & Preparation Worksheet
This worksheet is yours.

The questions below are prompts to help you gather your thoughts — not a questionnaire to hand in. You decide what to share in your meeting and what to keep private.

We've tried to balance the questions so you're not just looking at what's hard. We genuinely want to hear what's going well, what's bringing you life, and what God is doing in and through you. Start there if that helps.

Write freely and honestly — for yourself first. Pray before you start if that feels natural.

Let's start with the whole person — not just your role or your output.

Part 5
What Do You Need?
This is your chance to ask for what you actually need. Be specific where you can.
What is something leadership has done well that you want to name and affirm?
Positive feedback matters too — share it if it's true.
What is one thing leadership could do differently that would make a real difference for you?
Do you feel adequately equipped and supported for your work? Where are the gaps?
Have there been moments where you felt unseen or unsupported? Can you describe that?
This is safe to share. Leadership wants to know.
Would you find it valuable to connect with a counselor, spiritual director, or coach?
If yes — leadership would love to help make that happen. It's not weakness; it's good stewardship.
How is your fundraising support level? Any financial stressors we should know about?
