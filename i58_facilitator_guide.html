For internal use only · Confidential
Purpose & Philosophy

This is a pastoral conversation, not a performance review. You are here to see the whole person — their heart, their struggles, their growth, and how we can better support them. Come with genuine curiosity and care, not a checklist mentality.

Before You Begin — Facilitator Reminders
Pray before the meeting. Ask for wisdom, discernment, and genuine care for this person.
Review their pre-meeting worksheet before you sit down together.
Choose a private, comfortable space. Budget 60–90 minutes.
Listen more than you talk. Silence is okay — let them process.
Take notes directly in this guide, but don't let note-taking interrupt presence.
Follow the Spirit, not the script — let the conversation breathe.
Close the loop: follow up on every commitment you make.
Section 1
Team Care
Start here. This section builds trust and gets them talking openly before more structured topics.
Opening — How Are You, Really?
Give them space beyond 'fine.' If they give a surface answer, gently push: "What does that look like day to day?"
1.How have the last 6 months been for you overall — the highs and the lows?
2.What has felt most life-giving in this season?
3.What has been unexpectedly hard or draining?
Emotional & Spiritual Wellbeing
Watch for signs of burnout, compassion fatigue, or isolation.
4.How is your soul? Are you spiritually nourished, or running dry?
5.Are you finding rhythms of rest here in Germany?
6.Is there anything you've been carrying that you haven't had space to process?
Normalize this. Many volunteers carry more than they let on.
Team Dynamics
Explore both the positives and any tensions — don't only fish for problems.
7.What has been most encouraging or strengthening about the team in this season?
8.How would you describe the team culture right now?
9.Do you feel like you belong — known and not just functional?
10.Are there any relational tensions sitting with you that haven't been addressed?
If yes: "Have you been able to speak to it directly? How can we help?"
Section 2
Role & Fit
We want every volunteer in a role where they can thrive. This section is about clarity and alignment.
Role Clarity & Fit
1.How would you describe your current role? Does the description match the reality?
2.What parts of your role feel most natural and energizing?
3.What parts consistently feel like a poor fit or drain you?
Distinguish between healthy stretching and genuine misalignment.
4.Are there gifts or strengths you feel are underused right now?
5.If you could reshape your role in any way, what would you change?
Commitment & Engagement
If this person has a committed term end date, affirm it — this section is about engagement within that timeline, not pressure to extend.
6.Within your current commitment, how are you feeling about your engagement and sense of purpose?
7.Has anything shifted in your sense of calling or direction in the last 6 months?
Listen for uncertainty or drift — but also for renewed clarity.
8.Is there anything that would make your remaining time more sustainable or meaningful?
Section 3
Support & Resources
Come ready to make commitments. This section is about removing barriers and resourcing them well.
What They Need From Leadership
Be humble here. Receive feedback without defensiveness. Thank them.
1.What is one thing leadership could do differently that would make a real difference for you?
2.Do you feel adequately equipped and resourced for your work?
3.Have there been moments where you felt unsupported or unseen by leadership?
Ask: "What would have helped in that moment?"
4.Is there a decision you'd like to understand better or give input on?
External Support & Fundraising
Proactively offering outside support is a sign of care, not weakness.
5.Have you considered connecting with a counselor, spiritual director, or coach?
If open: "We'd love to help identify and fund that for you."
6.Are there professional development areas you'd like to grow in?
7.Do you have community outside of i58 here? (Church, friendships, expat connections?)
8.How is your fundraising support level — at, below, or above target?
9.Are there any financial stressors affecting your ability to focus on your work?
Section 4
Vision & Calling
Don't skip this section. It's often where the most important conversations happen.
Sense of Purpose & Direction
1.When you think about why you came here — what was your original calling? Does it still feel alive?
2.Do you feel like you know what God has you here for, or does it feel foggy?
If foggy — explore together. What would help bring clarity?
3.When do you feel most alive in this work? Describe a recent moment.
Engagement & Meaning
4.Is there anything about the work with migrants that has surprised or changed you?
5.Do you feel like your work is making a difference, even when it's hard to see?
6.Are there dreams or ideas you have that feel unexplored?
7.What would re-ignite your vision if you're feeling flat?
Closing & Next Steps

End every check-in with clear, specific commitments on both sides. Vague encouragement fades; concrete follow-through builds trust.

Closing Questions
1.Is there anything else on your heart that you haven't had space to say?
2.What is one thing you're asking God for in this next season?
3.What is one thing you need from us before our next check-in?
